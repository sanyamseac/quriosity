Quriosity

Play first.
Understand later. ✨

Alice and Bob walk you through what this hackathon is all about.

👩‍🚀
AliceHi! I'm Alice. We're making games where you accidentally learn quantum stuff.
🧑‍🔬
BobAnd I'm Bob. Yes, accidentally. Nobody reads a textbook at midnight, but everyone plays a game.
⚛️🎲🕹️
1 · The goal

Learn by playing

👩‍🚀
AliceThe goal is simple: a player finishes your game and suddenly gets a quantum idea.
🧑‍🔬
BobNo lecture. No quiz at the end. The fun is the lesson.
👩‍🚀
AliceThat "ohhh, so THAT'S how it works" feeling is what we're chasing.
2 · Priorities

Idea first, graphics second 🎨

🧑‍🔬
BobPlease don't spend 10 hours on shiny 3D models.
👩‍🚀
AliceA plain square that teaches something true beats a gorgeous game that teaches nothing.
🧑‍🔬
BobClear and correct wins. Pretty is a bonus.
3 · The heart of it

Build the game around the idea

👩‍🚀
AliceNot "a normal game with the word quantum stuck on it".
🧑‍🔬
BobThe rules themselves should behave like the real thing. If you remove the quantum idea, the game should fall apart.
👩‍🚀
AliceThink things being in two states at once, looking at something changing it, or objects linked across distance.
🌀 Superposition👀 Measurement🔗 Entanglement🚪 Gates
4 · Everyone's welcome

High-school brain? You're in 🎒

🧑‍🔬
BobIf a player needs a physics degree, we've lost them.
👩‍🚀
AliceKeep the math light. Let the gameplay explain things the way a good friend would.
5 · How to start

Learn. Struggle. Build. 🔨

👩‍🚀
AliceSpend the first few hours learning something you don't fully get yet.
🧑‍🔬
BobNotice what confuses you. That "wait, what?!" moment is gold.
👩‍🚀
AliceThen build a game that clears up that exact confusion for the next person.
📚 Learn😵 Struggle🛠️ Build
6 · Make it yours

Original & easy to play 🌐

🧑‍🔬
BobNo clones, no copies. Bring your own twist.
👩‍🚀
AliceAnd make it playable in a browser! Judges love clicking a link and playing right away. Hosting it online is even better.
🧑‍🔬
BobNow go make something that makes a quantum idea click. See you at the jam! 🚀
